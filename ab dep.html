ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Educational institution in Nedumangad, Kerala, offers
B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, MBA, and Hotel Management programs.
Established in 2002, it holds AICTE approval, APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University affiliation, and NAAC-A Grade accreditation.

The Department of Computer Applications is committed to excellence in education, research, and innovation. We offer industry-oriented programmes that equip students with technical expertise, problem-solving abilities, and leadership skills to thrive in the dynamic digital world. Our programmes MCA and BCA (Hons.) are designed to build strong foundations in computing, analytics, and business applications while encouraging creativity, collaboration, and ethical values.
