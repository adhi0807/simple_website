About the Department

The Department of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology is committed to providing quality education in the field of computer applications and information technology.

The department focuses on developing students' technical knowledge, programming skills, logical thinking, problem-solving abilities and professional skills. Students are provided with opportunities to gain both theoretical knowledge and practical experience in modern computing technologies.

The Department of Computer Applications offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, namely Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and Master of Computer Applications (MCA).

Courses Offered

BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications

BCA is an undergraduate programme that provides students with a strong foundation in computer applications and information technology.

Students learn programming, database management, web development, computer networks, software development and other important areas of computing.

MCA

Master of Computer Applications

MCA is a postgraduate programme designed to provide advanced knowledge and practical skills in computer applications and software development.

The programme covers areas such as software engineering, database management, artificial intelligence, data analytics and modern information technologies.
